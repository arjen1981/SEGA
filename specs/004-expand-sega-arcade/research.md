### Platform Notes

- All 8 new platforms share the same Wikipedia URL (the "List of Sega arcade system boards" article) since most individual platform pages redirect there.
- The **VCO Object**, **G80**, and **Sega Laserdisc** platforms are all very early (1981–1983) and provide historical depth.
- The **Sega System C** is essentially Mega Drive hardware in arcade form, making it unique as a "console-derived" arcade board.
- Three games (Zaxxon, Pengo, Congo Bongo) run on **documented custom boards** that are not named reusable systems. Per the amended spec edge case, these are included under identifiable SEGA arcade hardware. Recommendation: create a "Pre-System 1" platform node to cover these three games.
